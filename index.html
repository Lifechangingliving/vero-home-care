Our mission

Helping people live safely and confidently at home.

At Vero Home Care, our mission is to provide compassionate, dependable, and personalized care that empowers individuals to live safely, comfortably, and with dignity in the place they call home. We are committed to enhancing quality of life through exceptional service, meaningful relationships, and a person-centered approach that respects each individual’s unique needs. Through compassion, integrity, and excellence, we strive to give our clients and their families peace of mind, confidence, and the support they deserve.

Privacy & HIPAA

Your information is treated with care.

Vero Home Care respects and protects client information. Our team is required to keep client information confidential and follow all applicable privacy and HIPAA requirements. We do not sell or disclose client information except when authorized or required by law.

Caregiver standards

Trust begins before care starts.

Caregiver candidates complete required background and registry checks, employment and reference verification, orientation, and state-required training before providing services. Ongoing guidance reinforces safety, professionalism, privacy, and person-centered care.

Payment & benefit options

Let’s discuss the options available to you.

Potential payment and benefit sources may include private pay, Medicaid, private long-term care insurance, eligible Veterans benefits, workers’ compensation, and Area Agency on Aging grant programs.

Eligibility, authorization, coverage, funding, and Vero’s participation vary by program. Traditional Medicare generally does not cover ongoing non-medical personal care when it is the only service needed. Coverage is never guaranteed and should be confirmed before services begin.
